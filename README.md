**# Prodigy_Infotech_Internship_Task_1**
Using the US Census Demographics dataset to create a bar chart or histogram to visualize the distribution of a categorical or continuous variable.

**Data Visualization of US Census Demographics**
This project explores and visualizes demographic data from the US Census Bureau's 2017 ACS County Data (acs2017_county_data dataset). The focus is on creating meaningful visualizations that reveal trends and patterns within key demographic variables.

**Dataset**
The dataset used in this project is the acs2017_county_data from the US Census Bureau, which includes demographic information such as gender, income, and poverty levels across various counties in the United States.

**Visualizations**
In this project, we created the following visualizations to analyze and interpret the data:
1. **Bar Chart of Gender Ratio:**
    A bar chart representing the gender ratio across counties, providing insight into the distribution of male and female populations.

2. **Histogram of Median Household Income:**
    A histogram visualizing the distribution of median household incomes across counties, showing income spread and identifying income inequality patterns.

3. **Histogram of Poverty Percentage:**
    A histogram representing the percentage of individuals in poverty across counties, highlighting areas with higher poverty rates.

**Key Insights**
The gender ratio reveals areas where male and female populations are balanced or skewed.
The median household income distribution showcases regions with high-income disparities.
The poverty percentage histogram highlights counties with higher poverty rates, helping identify areas that may require more economic attention.

**Technologies Used**
Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
Jupyter Notebook

The dataset is included in the repository under the data/ directory. Alternatively, you can access it from the US Census Bureau website.
